# Reflex Damage Fix

Strudel mini album by **Eign0x**. Track 1, **No warning**, is the reference for the sound of tracks 2–5: dark sub and Reese bass, raw/Amen breaks, short synth phrases, and metallic accents.

Tracks 2–5 begin with atmosphere before the build and drop. Breaks change in scheduled phrases, with short fills and no stacked raw/Amen loops. Each track has its own bass rhythm and arrangement.

## Play / export

1. Open a single `.strudel.js` file and copy its entire contents into https://strudel.cc/.
2. Stop the previous pattern, then press Play. Allow the remote samples to load on the first play.
3. For a complete export, use the range above with `START_CYCLE = 0` and `LOOP = false`.

One cycle is four beats. The export ranges include four cycles after the arrangement for effect tails. Section positions are listed at the top of each new file. `START_CYCLE` lets you audition a later section.

Samples are loaded from the public TidalCycles Dirt-Samples repository. Internet access is required for the initial load. No other track file is required to play a track.

Track 1 is preserved exactly as supplied. Tracks 2–5 use a separate master level suitable for their new arrangements.
